# Kellogg Antarctica Trek

A zero-to-one experiential travel product created for Kellogg MBA students and designed around adventure, community, leadership, and climate awareness.

I conceived and launched the initiative after identifying an opportunity to create a distinctive, once-in-a-lifetime experience for the Kellogg community.

The project progressed from initial concept and demand validation through partnership development, pricing, digital product design, participant recruitment, and launch.

## Overview

The Kellogg Antarctica Trek was built as a complete participant experience rather than only a group trip.

The product included:

* A defined experience concept and positioning
* A dedicated digital platform
* Participant onboarding
* Pricing and financial planning
* Expedition-operator partnership
* Recruitment and communication channels
* Travel guidance and participant resources
* Community-building initiatives

## The User Problem

MBA students have access to many traditional travel experiences, but few combine:

* Adventure
* Leadership
* Community
* Global exposure
* Climate awareness
* A distinctive shared experience

The goal was to create a differentiated experience that students would value before, during, and after the expedition.

## Product Development Process

### 1. Opportunity Identification

Identified an unmet opportunity for a premium, student-led Antarctica experience within the Kellogg community.

### 2. Demand Validation

Tested interest across the student community and generated demand from more than 150 prospective participants.

### 3. Product Definition

Defined the experience proposition, participant profile, itinerary structure, accommodation options, communication model, and overall customer journey.

### 4. Partnership Development

Negotiated an exclusive arrangement with an Antarctic expedition operator, including preferred pricing and dedicated participant capacity.

### 5. Digital Experience

Designed and developed the public website, participant playbook, and digital communication channels.

### 6. Launch and Recruitment

Launched the program, recruited 16 MBA participants, and generated approximately $120,000 in expedition bookings.

## Key Outcomes

* More than 150 prospective participants expressed interest
* 16 MBA participants joined the inaugural cohort
* Approximately $120,000 in expedition bookings
* Exclusive expedition-operator partnership established
* Public website and participant playbook launched
* Recruitment, onboarding, and communication systems created
* Foundation established for a repeatable annual Kellogg experience
